# Enterprise Knowledge Hub (EKH) 🚀

An AI-powered enterprise knowledge management platform that transforms scattered organizational documents into a centralized, intelligent, and searchable knowledge system.

Enterprise Knowledge Hub leverages Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to allow employees to interact with company knowledge through natural-language conversations and receive context-aware, source-cited answers.

### 🌐 Live Demo
https://enterprise-knowledge-hub-puce.vercel.app

### 🎯 Key Capabilities

- **Intelligent Document Management:** Upload and manage enterprise documents.
- **AI-Powered Conversational Search:** Ask questions and retrieve relevant information using natural language.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** Generate answers grounded in organizational documents.
- **Hybrid Search:** Combine semantic and keyword-based retrieval.
- **Source Citations:** Trace AI-generated answers back to their supporting documents.
- **Role-Based Access Control:** Control access to organizational knowledge.
- **Multi-Department Support:** Organize knowledge across different business departments.
- **Document Processing:** Extract, process, and index PDF and DOCX documents.
- **Secure Knowledge Retrieval:** Ensure users retrieve only information they are authorized to access.

### 💼 Business Use Cases

- Human Resources and employee policies.
- Technical documentation and engineering knowledge.
- Finance and company compliance documents.
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
- Internal company knowledge bases.
- Employee onboarding and organizational support.

### 🚀 Vision

To build a centralized AI knowledge ecosystem that helps organizations reduce information silos, improve employee productivity, accelerate decision-making, and make enterprise knowledge accessible through secure and intelligent AI interactions.
